Generated by `scripts/make_tables.py`; all estimators are run by the C++ executable. No fitting to benchmark thresholds occurs. The [weighted literature references](../literature/) are computed separately from published estimates and nominal errors.

These methods are distinct approximations, not a hierarchy of guarantees. High-dimensional truncations can deteriorate badly at small d; their failure is retained here. A quoted Monte Carlo or series uncertainty is not a rigorous enclosure. Empty cells mean unsupported method or no verified reference.
